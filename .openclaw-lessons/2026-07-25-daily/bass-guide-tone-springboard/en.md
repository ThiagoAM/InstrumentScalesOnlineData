# Bass guide-tone springboard

<!-- block -->

Loop **Dm7 - G7 - Cmaj7** at **88 bpm**. The springboard idea is simple: use roots on beat 1, then jump toward a **3rd** or **7th** before the bar ends so the next chord feels invited in.

<!-- block -->

Round 1: play one bar per chord with **quarter notes** only. Strong landings are **F** or **C** on Dm7, **B** or **F** on G7, and **E** or **B** on Cmaj7.

<!-- block -->

Round 2: in at least two bars, use one **chromatic approach note** on beat 4 before the guide tone. Keep the line singing, not academic; if it sounds stiff, repeat with fewer notes.

<!-- block -->

Challenge round: write a 6-bar line that includes one leap bigger than a 4th, two chromatic approaches, and one bar where beat 3 is silent but the time still feels solid. Then loop it until the chord changes sound obvious without a backing track.

<!-- block -->

Checkpoint: Play one clean 3-bar pass that hits a guide tone in every bar, including one chromatic approach on beat 4.
