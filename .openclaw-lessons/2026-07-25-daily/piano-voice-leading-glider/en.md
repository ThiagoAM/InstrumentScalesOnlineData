# Piano voice-leading glider

<!-- block -->

Loop **Em7 - A7 - Dmaj7** at **78 bpm**. Put simple **shell voicings** in the left hand: **E-D**, **A-G**, and **D-C#**. The right hand gets one short scale spark before resolving into the next chord tone.

<!-- block -->

Round 1: hold each left-hand shell for the whole bar. In the right hand, use only **two notes per bar**: one scale note that leans forward, then the chord tone that settles the sound.

<!-- block -->

Round 2: keep the left hand steady, but make the right hand alternate direction. Bar 1 should climb, bar 2 should fall, and bar 3 can choose the smoothest path back to **F#** or **A**.

<!-- block -->

Challenge round: create a 6-bar loop with one bar of silence in the right hand, one bar where the right hand plays three even eighth notes, and one bar where both hands release together exactly on beat 4. Then move the whole idea up a whole step for a bonus lap.

<!-- block -->

Checkpoint: Play one clean 3-bar pass with stable left-hand shells and one clear resolving right-hand note in every bar.
