# Guitar resolution slalom

<!-- block -->

Loop **Am7 - D7** at **82 bpm** and stay around **5th position**. Your slalom gates are **C** over Am7 and **F#** over D7; each bar can wander, but the finish line must sound settled.

<!-- block -->

Round 1: use only **quarter notes** and approach the target from one scale step above or below. Over Am7 you can drift through **A Dorian**, but bar 1 has to land on **C**. Over D7, bar 2 has to land on **F#**.

<!-- block -->

Round 2: keep the same targets, but make bar 1 mostly ascending and bar 2 mostly descending. If your phrase gets crowded, remove notes instead of rushing.

<!-- block -->

Challenge round: build a 4-bar phrase where one landing uses a slide, one landing uses a rest before beat 4, and the final landing is longer than the others. Then improvise freely for one minute and keep the move that makes the resolutions feel inevitable.

<!-- block -->

Checkpoint: Play one clean 2-bar pass that lands on C over Am7 and F# over D7 with steady time.
