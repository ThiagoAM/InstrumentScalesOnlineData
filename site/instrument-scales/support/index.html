Help with Instrument Scales on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Email support

Include the device, operating system, app version, the feature you used and what you expected. A screenshot with personal details removed can help. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

I cannot hear the notes

Check the output device and volume, then try a note in Explore. If headphones or Bluetooth audio are connected, check where the sound is going. Describe which notes or screen are silent when contacting support.

My Pro or Max purchase is missing

Use the same Apple Account that purchased the product. Open the app’s plans screen and choose Restore Purchases. An active Max subscription includes shared Premium features; Pro remains a separate one-time purchase.

How do I change or cancel Max?

Apple manages billing and subscriptions. Follow Apple’s subscription instructions below. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I want to request a refund

Refund requests are handled by Apple. Use the official refund instructions below; eligibility is decided by Apple.

A lesson or tuning seems wrong

Tell us the lesson title, instrument, tuning and exact notes or positions. A visual example helps us reproduce a musical inconsistency.

Can I study offline?

Downloaded or cached lesson availability can depend on the app version and which material has been opened. Open the material with a connection before travelling. If a lesson cannot load, tell us its title and app version.
