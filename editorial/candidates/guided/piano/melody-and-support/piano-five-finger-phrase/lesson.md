# A five-finger phrase

Play C4–D4–E4–G4–F4–E4–D4–C4 with right-hand fingers 1–2–3–5–4–3–2–1.

Use a keyboard with C3 through C6 available; these examples stay within that region. C4 is middle C. Start without pedal and release any tension before continuing.

:::locale pt-BR
# Uma frase com cinco dedos

Toque C4–D4–E4–G4–F4–E4–D4–C4 com dedos 1–2–3–5–4–3–2–1 da direita.

Use teclado com C3 até C6; os exemplos ficam nessa região. C4 é dó central. Comece sem pedal e solte qualquer tensão antes de continuar.

:::locale es
# Una frase de cinco dedos

Toca C4–D4–E4–G4–F4–E4–D4–C4 con dedos derechos 1–2–3–5–4–3–2–1.

Usa teclado con C3 a C6; los ejemplos quedan en esa región. C4 es do central. Empieza sin pedal y suelta cualquier tensión.

:::locale de
# Eine Fünffingerphrase

Spiele C4–D4–E4–G4–F4–E4–D4–C4 rechts mit 1–2–3–5–4–3–2–1.

Nutze eine Tastatur mit C3 bis C6; die Beispiele bleiben dort. C4 ist mittleres C. Beginne ohne Pedal und löse Spannung vor dem Weiterüben.

:::locale ja
# 5本の指のフレーズ

右手1–2–3–5–4–3–2–1でC4–D4–E4–G4–F4–E4–D4–C4を弾きます。

C3からC6まである鍵盤を使います。C4は中央Cです。例はこの範囲内です。ペダルなしで始め、緊張を解いてから続けます。

:::locale zh-Hans
# 五指乐句

右手用1–2–3–5–4–3–2–1弹C4–D4–E4–G4–F4–E4–D4–C4。

键盘需有C3到C6，示例在此范围。C4是中央C。不用踏板开始，先放松紧张再继续。
:::endlocalized

:::step id=practice-and-feedback phase=practice
:::localized
:::locale en
Practise this 3-beat cell twice at 48 BPM. First play without the demonstration; then compare the note order, rests and arrival with the written reference. If either attempt changes the pulse, stop and isolate its difficult transition.

If finger 4 lands late, isolate G4–F4–E4 slowly without pressing harder.

:::locale pt-BR
Pratique esta célula de 3 tempos duas vezes a 48 BPM. Primeiro toque sem demonstração; compare depois ordem das notas, pausas e chegada com a referência escrita. Se uma tentativa mudar o pulso, pare e isole a transição difícil.

Se o dedo 4 atrasar, isole G4–F4–E4 devagar sem apertar mais.

:::locale es
