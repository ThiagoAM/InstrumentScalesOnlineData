Shape the solo-forming turn pass through its opening, space, contour, and ending; do not fill every beat. Use turn check on the eight-bar sketch turn pass to decide if it sounds like one thought; its rests should feel counted and its ending chosen, not accidental.

To review the eight-bar sketch turn pass, isolate the last rise and first fall, then reconnect them without a pause. Record two turn pass takes with eight-bar sketch at 71 BPM. Accept the solo-forming result, two clean turns, when both keep the same opening, intentional rests, and clear ending without extra filler.
